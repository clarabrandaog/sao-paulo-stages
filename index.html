Four districts hold 44% of São Paulo's theatre rooms

São Paulo is the hub of Brazil's musical theatre: an economic impact study by SBTM found that the city's 28 musical productions of 2018 generated R$1.01 billion and 12,824 jobs. Yet its stages are packed into a few central districts around Avenida Paulista and the old downtown, while most of the outer districts have none. This map crosses the city's official count of theatre rooms per district with the venues the OpenStreetMap community has placed on the map, to show where the city's stages are and where the record of them is thin.

About the data

District counts of theatre rooms and seats are from 2017, the latest year the city publishes per district as a table, from Infocidade (Prefeitura de São Paulo, SMUL/Geoinfo, sourced from the Secretaria Municipal de Cultura and the Guia da Folha). A building with several stages counts once per stage.

Venues

Venue points are every place tagged as a theatre on OpenStreetMap inside the city, retrieved in November 2026 through the Overpass API. Unnamed points, a buffet, a samba school, circus tents, university lecture rooms and duplicates were removed, leaving 85 venues. OpenStreetMap is edited by volunteers, so it is incomplete: some major houses are missing.

Shapes and method

District boundaries are from GeoSampa, via codigourbano/distritos-sp, simplified in mapshaper and stored as TopoJSON. Each venue is assigned to the district that contains it. Built with D3 by Clara Brandão, Parsons MFA Design and Technology.
